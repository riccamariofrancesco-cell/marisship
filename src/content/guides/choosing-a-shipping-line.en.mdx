A **shipping line** is a combination of carriers and route that the agent offers to take your parcel from its warehouse to your door. Every agent has several, from express couriers to "special" lines for certain goods or countries, all the way to sea freight. Picking only by the lowest price in the table is the quickest way to regret it.

## The checklist

1. **Total price for your chargeable weight.** Compare lines at the same weight, including fuel surcharge, fees and discounts. That is exactly what the [calculator](/en/shipping-calculator) is for.
2. **Transit time.** Look at the stated range and, if you can, at recent experiences of other customers shipping to your country.
3. **Tracking.** Some lines show every step, others go silent for days after departure.
4. **Weight and size limits.** Many lines have a maximum weight per parcel and limits on the longest side.
5. **Accepted goods.** Batteries, liquids, cosmetics, magnets, food and powders are often forbidden or allowed only on specific lines.
6. **Taxes and customs clearance.** With a **DDP** line (Delivered Duty Paid) taxes are paid in advance; with a **DAP** line (Delivered At Place) you pay them on delivery, often with a courier fee for clearance.
7. **Insurance and claims.** Read what it covers, up to what value, and how to file a claim.
8. **The last-mile carrier.** Knowing who delivers in your country helps you predict the timing and reliability of the final leg.

## Comparing fairly

- Use **the same chargeable weight** for every line: some use different volumetric divisors.
- Check **the date** of the rates: they change often, especially fuel surcharges.
- Don't forget **payment fees**: they apply to shipping too.

## One thing not to do

Declaring a lower value than the real one to customs is illegal and can void the insurance if the parcel is lost or damaged. Declare the actual value: it is the only way to be covered.

The calculator's comparison table only shows verified rates, with date and source. If you have up-to-date ones, you can [suggest them on GitHub](https://github.com/riccamariofrancesco-cell/marisship/blob/main/src/data/shipping-lines.json).
