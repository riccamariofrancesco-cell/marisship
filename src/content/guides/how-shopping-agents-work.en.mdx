Many shops on Taobao, Weidian and 1688 don't ship abroad and don't take foreign cards. A **shopping agent** solves that: it is a company based in China that buys on your behalf, receives the goods at its warehouse and then ships them to you in a single parcel.

## The journey of an order

1. **Paste the item link** on the agent's website (the [Marisship link converter](/en/link-converter) gives you the right link for every supported agent).
2. **Pay for the item** plus domestic shipping to the warehouse, and possibly a service fee.
3. **The seller ships to the agent's warehouse.** This usually takes one to five days.
4. **The agent checks the goods**: many send quality-control (QC) photos before shipping. This is the moment to ask the seller for an exchange or a return.
5. **Build your parcel**: choose which items ship together, packaging options and the shipping line.
6. **Pay for international shipping**, based on the real weight of the parcel weighed at the warehouse.
7. **Receive the parcel** and pay import taxes where due.

So there are **two** payments: one for the items and, later, one for shipping. The second is the one that brings the surprises.

## Where costs hide

- **The exchange rate.** Agents convert yuan into euros or dollars at their own rate, sometimes worse than the official one.
- **Payment fees.** Cards, PayPal and other methods can cost a few percent.
- **The service fee.** Some agents charge none, others take a percentage of the order.
- **Volumetric weight.** A light but bulky parcel is priced like a heavy one. See our guide on [actual vs volumetric weight](/en/guides/actual-vs-volumetric-weight).
- **Extras**: reinforced packaging, insurance, extra photos, storage beyond the free days.
- **Import taxes.** In the European Union, since 1 July 2021 VAT is charged on all imported goods, however low their value, and since 1 July 2026 low-value parcels also pay a flat customs duty. Amounts and rules can change: always check [Your Europe](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/vat/index_en.htm) and the [European Commission's page on buying from outside the EU](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/buying-goods-online-coming-non-european-union-country_en). Outside the EU, check your own customs authority.

The Marisship [shipping calculator](/en/shipping-calculator) lines up weight, surcharges, discounts and fees so you see the real total before you pay.

## Before you pick an agent

- Check **which shipping lines** it offers to your country, and their weight limits.
- Read **recent reviews**, not just the ones featured on its website.
- Find out **how many days of storage** are free and what happens afterwards.
- See **how it handles returns and problems** with sellers.
- Compare the **total**, not a single line item: a low fee can hide a poor exchange rate.

> An agent ships what you order, but you remain responsible for what you import. Counterfeit goods can be seized by customs, and in some countries, Italy among them, buyers can be fined. Marisship is built for legitimate purchases.
