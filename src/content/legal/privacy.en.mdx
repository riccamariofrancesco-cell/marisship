Marisship is an independent, non-commercial project. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

## What we don't do

- **No cookies.** The site sets no cookies, not even for language.
- **No tracking or analytics**, no advertising, no profiling.
- **No accounts**: we never ask for your name, email or any other data.

## What stays in your browser

The **Hold**, your **preferred agent** and the light or dark **theme** are saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage), on your device. They are not sent to us. You can delete them at any time by emptying the Hold or clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

## When our server works for you

- **Short links.** When you convert an official marketplace short link (for example m.tb.cn), the link is sent to our server, which opens it to find out which item it points to. We don't store it.
- **Exchange rates.** Our server downloads the ECB reference rates from Frankfurter once a day. Your browser never contacts that service.
- **Fonts.** Fonts are served from our own domain: your browser doesn't contact Google.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by **Vercel Inc.** Like any web server, Vercel's infrastructure processes technical request data (such as IP address, time and page requested) to run and protect the service, and keeps it for a limited time under its own [privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## External links and GitHub

When you open a link to a marketplace or an agent, their privacy rules apply. If you open a GitHub issue to contact us, GitHub's rules apply and the issue is **public**: don't include personal data.

## Your rights

As we don't collect personal data, we have no data to show you or delete. For any question you can [reach us via GitHub](/en/contact). You always have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
